# Neon Manufacturing Guidelines

Source: `C:\Users\Ryzen\Downloads\GUIDELINE DESIGN.pdf`

Apply these rules to every future neon mockup and production artwork:

1. Trace the requested design first. After tracing, check all manufacturing limitations and keep the adjusted result as similar to the original as possible.
2. Use a minimum stroke/tube thickness of 6 mm.
3. Use a minimum circle diameter of 4 cm.
4. Use a minimum line or tube-segment length of 2 cm.
5. Do not use tight sharp angles. Convert them into curves. A sharp angle is acceptable only when its degree/opening is sufficiently wide.
6. Double-line fonts require a minimum letter size of 9 cm.
7. If a double-line font becomes too small, convert it to a single-line treatment. The single-line minimum size is 5 cm.
8. Avoid long tube intersections or long overlapping paths.
9. Do not make a short curve too steep. Open and soften the curve.
10. If a letter contains a circle/counter that becomes too small, replace the small circle with a straight-line treatment that preserves the letter's appearance.
11. Small gaps between lines can fail during production. Widen narrow gaps.

## Configurator interpretation

- Font previews are visual suggestions, not production-ready tube paths.
- The final mockup must be traced and checked against every rule above.
- If a selected font cannot meet the limits at the final physical size, preserve its character while simplifying it to a manufacturable single-line or wider-gap version.
